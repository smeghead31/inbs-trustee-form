How your information is used

From early 2026 charity trustee first and last names are displayed on the public Scottish Charity Register, unless an exemption has been approved. OSCR removes a former trustee's details from the Register when they cease to be a trustee. Trustee information is held in OSCR's internal schedule for two years after a person ceases to be a trustee, after which it is deleted. Contact details, date of birth and home address are not published.

Before you answer

In certain circumstances a charity trustee can have their name exempted from publication on the Scottish Charity Register. An exemption applies only after an application has been approved by OSCR, and is granted only where specific criteria are met. Guidance is available on the OSCR website. Answer Yes below only if you intend to apply.
